Where small PM teams quietly lose their week

With fewer than 25 people covering hundreds of doors, every manual handoff compounds. These are the usual culprits.

Maintenance requests that live in texts and email

Tenants reach out five different ways, vendors get dispatched from memory, and nobody can say where a work order stands without asking around.

Leasing leads that go cold

Inquiries from listings and your website wait for someone to copy them into a spreadsheet, and the prospect rents somewhere else first.

Owner reports built by hand

Every month, someone re-keys numbers from three places into a report owners may or may not read.

Move-ins, renewals and rent follow-up

Lease dates, reminders and late-rent nudges depend on someone remembering, so things slip when the team gets busy.
